## Outcome and scope

- Publish persisted successful answers only to explicitly opted-in compatible/authorized brokers under meshcore/client/{PUBLIC_KEY}/regions.
- Implement the verified CoreScope client contract using lowercase reporting Companion/target keys, observation timestamp, regions, truncated, and optional repeater_clock.
- Preserve successful data and the observation time across delayed publication/failures; implement the approved retry/restart policy.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Outbound payloads and broker opt-in configuration use centralized strict AJV schemas and existing MQTT seams.
- [ ] One broker failure does not erase data, stop capture, or force publication to other brokers.
- [ ] Topic identity, timestamp units, empty answers, wildcard/case, and completeness limitations match the verified contract.
- [ ] CoreScope enablement and broker permission prerequisites are documented; GPS, RF samples, and observer /neighbors are not added.
- [ ] Publication after restart/delay preserves the measured answer semantics.

### Passive topology protocol subset — 2026-10-08

[Scoped protocol notes](../topology-protocol-notes.md) record installed @liamcottle/meshcore.js 1.15.0 and primary firmware a366955cb2f67b8e6842d4f00d2b6a554dddd88a (2026-09-30). Width/count encoding, 64-byte effective path limit, flood append order, consumed direct paths, TRACE SNR semantics, transport-code offsets and public-key prefix identity are verified for #27 planning. Synthetic header fixture specifications cover 1/2/3-byte/scoped/direct/empty/truncated cases; implementations follow approval of #27's storage/configuration plan.

These findings support the passive path subset without upgrading dependencies or testing hardware. They do not verify deployed firmware, unknown-node prefix uniqueness or a reversible outbound route. The full remote status/telemetry/neighbour/region/authentication/contact/correlation matrix remains incomplete, so #22 stays open/In progress with its acceptance boxes unchanged.

Primary references at that exact firmware ref: [Mesh forwarding and TRACE](https://github.com/meshcore-dev/MeshCore/blob/a366955cb2f67b8e6842d4f00d2b6a554dddd88a/src/Mesh.cpp), [Packet route/width definitions](https://github.com/meshcore-dev/MeshCore/blob/a366955cb2f67b8e6842d4f00d2b6a554dddd88a/src/Packet.h), [Identity prefix matching](https://github.com/meshcore-dev/MeshCore/blob/a366955cb2f67b8e6842d4f00d2b6a554dddd88a/src/Identity.h), [Dispatcher validation](https://github.com/meshcore-dev/MeshCore/blob/a366955cb2f67b8e6842d4f00d2b6a554dddd88a/src/Dispatcher.cpp), [64-byte path / 184-byte payload / 255-byte frame bounds](https://github.com/meshcore-dev/MeshCore/blob/a366955cb2f67b8e6842d4f00d2b6a554dddd88a/src/MeshCore.h), and [packet format](https://github.com/meshcore-dev/MeshCore/blob/a366955cb2f67b8e6842d4f00d2b6a554dddd88a/docs/packet_format.md). Installed library evidence: src/packet.js, meshcore_path.js and buffer_reader.js under @liamcottle/meshcore.js 1.15.0. Its isRouteFlood predicate only covers unscoped flood and readBytes can return short slices; the consumer must validate both explicitly.
